Section 1: Tokenizer

TEXT → NUMBERS

LLMs cannot read raw characters directly. They break text into discrete tokens and assign each a unique numerical ID from a fixed vocabulary.

What You Learn: Tokenization & Vocabulary

Before an LLM processes meaning, text undergoes tokenization. Common words become individual tokens with fixed numeric IDs. Unseen words are mapped to [UNK], just as a simple demo vocabulary would handle novel input.

Section 2: Embeddings in 3D

NUMBERS → GEOMETRY

Token IDs are converted into continuous vectors. In vector space, sentences with similar meanings or shared semantic contexts land close to each other.

What You Learn: Vector Embeddings & High-Dimensional Space

Real LLMs use embeddings with thousands of dimensions (e.g. 1536 or 4096 dimensions). Here we project them into 3D. Sentences with shared concepts become nearby points in the vector space.
